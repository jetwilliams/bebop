The owner reads replies on a phone, often between other things. Lead with the answer or the result. Keep each
message to a few short lines. No wide tables (they wrap badly on a phone); use short lists. Ask one question at a
time, and make it answerable with a word or two.

**Why:** the owner said long replies get skimmed and the question at the end gets missed (2026-10-01).

**How to apply:** every Telegram reply. Longer write-ups go in a file; send the path and a two-line summary.
